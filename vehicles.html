Typical Vehicles in Southern and Northern Ghana

The Wikimedia Commons has over 100 million images on almost anything you can imagine. While putting together this website, I realized that I had not captured photos of the vehicles from which I had collected sayings. To make the terms ‘tro-tro’ and ‘tricycle’ clear, I searched for those terms on the Wikimedia Commons to share some pictures of each without running afoul of copyright issues. In fact, I noticed that such pictures also include the sayings, although that was not the reason those photos were taken.
