LET THEM SAY George Bernard Shaw modernizes 1593 motto "Thay Haif Said: Quhat Say Thay? Lat Thame Say." to "They say. What do they say? Let them say." Encouragement to let your opponent state their case, without having to respond. Roger David Hardesty, a historian from Portland, Oregon, explains this saying in his blog post if you want to really get into it.
